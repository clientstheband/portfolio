Status: Obscured

This is artwork & other visual materials for an elusive record called ‘Here Comes the Flood’ by a band called Clients, who supposedly formed in the city of Hobart, Tasmania, sometime in 2013. This project included album artwork and design, record packaging, promotional material, merchandising, and a static website. The album cover is made of gouache paints and correction fluid; the website is made of html.
